# The first battle actually played — 2026-08-01

Until this session nobody had measured how a battle in this game **runs**, only how it is built.
A real 7v7 was driven from a scripted input sequence through the full timer:
`LocalAuthoritativeServer::new_random_7v7`, seed 7, Bystra Valley, player in a T-54, 25 200 ticks.

Harness: `crates/apps/client/examples/play_session.rs` (temporary — see `README.md`).

Three of the four findings below are **invisible in the source**.

---

## 1. Zero ricochets — the core promise never fires

**53 damage events. 53 penetrations. Not one ricochet.**

```
t1462   4 -> 12   320 hp  pen=true ric=false Some(Engine)
t1967   4 -> 12   320 hp  pen=true ric=false Some(AmmoRack)
t2349   8 -> 1    240 hp  pen=true ric=false Some(Engine)
t2472   4 -> 12   320 hp  pen=true ric=false Some(AmmoRack)
t2553  10 ->  6   240 hp  pen=true ric=false None
t2829   2 -> 10   390 hp  pen=true ric=false Some(Gun)
...  53 events, every one pen=true, none ric=true
```

A full seven-minute battle in which real 3D plates, true angle of incidence and ricochet — the
identity of this project — did their job **zero times**.

**Consequence: angling, hull-down and aim-point selection are dead mechanics.** There is currently
no difference between hitting the glacis and hitting the flank.

Hypotheses, unresolved and worth separating before any fix:

- bots engage at close range and near-perpendicular angles (`bot_combat_command` is stand-and-shoot
  on the nearest visible enemy, so they rarely present or exploit obliquity);
- fleet penetration is simply too high against fleet armour;
- the ricochet threshold (a hard step at 70°, `resolve.rs:230-238`) is never reached in practice.

## 2. The battle does not resolve inside its own timer

Seven minutes: **7 of 14 destroyed, `outcome: None`.**

At this rate a 7v7 needs roughly fourteen minutes against a seven-minute limit, so **every battle
would end on the clock with half the roster alive** — the least satisfying possible outcome, and
one that makes any balance judgement impossible.

Deaths, in order: t3581 (the player), t4016, t5120, t8456, t10373, t12287, t13148.

## 3. The bots barely fight

First 60 seconds: **16 hits and one death — the player**, who was driving in a straight line and
firing blind. Thirteen bots killed nobody in a minute.

They DO concentrate on a visible moving target: four different bots finished the player over about
twenty seconds (t2349, t2828, t3309, t3381, t3448, t3581). But left alone they do not seek combat.
This matches the audit: target selection is `bot_nearest_engageable_enemy` and nothing else — no
threat priority, no finishing a cripple, no answering the tank shooting at you, no withdrawal.

**Module damage dominates**: 16 of the 24 logged events destroyed or damaged a module, mostly the
engine. Consistent with AP dealing ×1.0 of its alpha to modules (`resolve.rs:250-263`), and with the
earlier finding that a single frontal penetration can knock out a gun.

## 4. Bug — the camera freezes when the player dies

Four rendered frames from t5042 to t20162 are **pixel-identical** while the battle continues around
them. `tick_death_spectate` exists in `app/camera_link.rs` but does not drive this path.

## Correction to an earlier claim in this audit

The muzzle flash is derived from a jump in `reload_remaining_s` between snapshots
(`fx/fire.rs:1-5`), and I claimed that meant "≤50 ms from the 20 Hz snapshot cadence". Measured:

```
fired t509   revealed t510    16.7 ms
fired t1016  revealed t1017   16.7 ms
fired t1523  revealed t1524   16.7 ms
...
n=7  mean=16.7 ms  min=16.7  max=16.7
```

**One tick. Zero variance.** The claim was inferred from `DEFAULT_SNAPSHOT_HZ`, not measured — the
local snapshot lands on the firing tick. **In local play this is imperceptible, and the priority of
predicting the shot drops accordingly.**

What still stands: the cue is DERIVED rather than PREDICTED, so networked play adds RTT on top of
it; and `fire.rs:27` requires `latest.hit_points > 0`, so a tank that fires and dies inside one
snapshot window shows no muzzle flash at all — the loudest shot in a battle is invisible.
