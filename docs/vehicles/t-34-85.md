# T-34-85 — reference dossier

Researched 2026-09-06 (Forge 2.0 K3, the "dossier and measure" pass this file owed since its
2026-08-03 stub). Every number below carries its source; the reference pack
(`vehicle_forge/reference/t34_85.reference.ron`) now locks the dimension anchors, and the part

## Identity

The **1944 production T-34-85 with the 85 mm ZiS-S-53** (L/54.6), in the **Factory 183** (Nizhny
Tagil) configuration: the single-piece "flattened" turret casting of the design's origin — no cheek
bulges — over the welded 45 mm hull. What this model deliberately is NOT: the early D-5T-armed run
(January–March 1944; a longer 8.30 m overall and the D-5T's mantlet), the Factory 112 "composite"
turret (two castings joined by a vertical weld with pronounced cheek bulges — the hobby literature's
"composite" is 112, its "flattened" is 183), and the post-war T-34-85M (DShK ring mount, two 200 L
drums). The commander's cupola keeps the two-leaf folding hatch (single-leaf only from January 1945).

## Form rules (what makes it *this* tank)

- **One 45 mm plate everywhere, sloped**: the 60° glacis with the driver's hatch cut INTO it, the
  40° raked upper sides overhanging the tracks, the 47° stern — a welded hull of rolled plate.
- **The Factory-183 flattened turret**: a low single-piece casting seated forward on the 1.60 m
  race, the ZiS-S-53's rounded cast mantlet, the commander's cupola aft with FIVE vision slits and
  the two-leaf hatch, the loader's rectangular hatch on the roof right, TWO bulbous ventilator
  domes at the roof's rear, infantry handrails on the walls.
- **Christie running gear**: five 830 mm rubber-rimmed spider wheels a side on coil springs (no
  torsion bars), the open gap behind the first station where the spring well bulges the hull side,
  no return rollers, the sprocket at the rear, 500 mm waffle tracks.
- **The bow**: the DT ball mount right of the driver, the single headlight on the glacis left, cast
  tow hooks with spring catches at the glacis and stern corners, full-length mudguards curved at
  both ends carrying the fuel drums and tool boxes; twin round exhaust outlets low on the stern.
- What it does NOT carry: a muzzle brake or evacuator, an AA machine gun (the DShK ring is the
  post-war T-34-85M's), smoke canisters, side skirts.

## Known deviations & follow-ups

- The turret front's 28° facet and the 2.64 × 2.20 plan are modelling approximations (no source
  gives the casting's plan or an angle); the cupola's size and station and the 1.90 m fire line are
  unsourced — a camera-match against a museum photo scaled by the 1.60 m ring or the 0.83 m wheel.
- The apex anchor (2.71) stays `Target` until the library builds the cupola to the authored height.
- The K0 outlines: the two PD SVGs need rasterizing (no SVG rasterizer on the build machine yet).
