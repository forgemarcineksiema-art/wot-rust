# T-54 Benchmark Vehicle — reference dossier

## Identity

- Implemented production variant: **`T-54-3 obr. 1951`** (production Nov 1951 – May 1955, 7009
  built). The classic fully hemispherical turret without заман (undercut) and without a rear
  niche — undercuts were eliminated in stages: obr. 1947 (T-34-85-like turret, front+rear shot
  traps) → obr. 1949 (dome with flat lower sides, rear overhang kept) → obr. 1951 (clean dome).
- Deliberately NOT this model: T-54A (1955: D-10TG with bore evacuator, STP-1 stabilizer, second
  IR lamp), T-54B (1957: D-10T2S, STP-2, IR night set), T-55 (different height/ammo/fuel-rack
  anatomy — a frequent source of contaminated reference numbers, see Height below).
- This is the canonical Armored Vehicle Forge benchmark, judged module by module. The former
  `T-55A` clone was **removed entirely** from `VehicleKind` (protocol v33, no-clones rule).

## Form rules (what makes it *this* tank)

1. Full hemispherical cast dome, no undercut anywhere, no rear bustle/niche.
2. Five cast road wheels per side, ⌀810 mm, twin discs with a ~53 mm gap between the tyres,
   the 1st↔2nd gap visibly enlarged; no return rollers — the top run lies on the wheels;
   rear sprocket (2 × 13 teeth), front idler with its two-worm tension crank.
   (Disc pattern: spider-web on the as-built obr. 1951 per S1b — see Part construction.)
3. Plain tapering D-10T barrel — any muzzle furniture (evacuator, counterweight) marks a later
   variant and is wrong here.
4. Narrow gun embrasure with the mantlet inside and a canvas cover outside — the "pig's head"
   external ball is a WoT-ism, not obr. 1951.
5. Commander cupola LEFT, loader hatch RIGHT with the DShK-M ring mount on the hatch.
6. Fender asymmetry: right fender carries two flat rectangular fuel tanks + stowage; left
   carries three stowage bins; exhaust rear-left.
7. Single white headlamp on the glacis, left; fixed SG-43 course-MG port in the glacis right of
   center; 2× MDSh smoke canisters on the rear plate; unditching log; **no external gun travel
   lock**; **no rear 200 L fuel drums** (brackets appeared ~1959).

## Gameplay translation

- Modules: hull 100/80/45, turret 200/90/65 (side taper 160→65 lands with PR-09), V-54 390 kW,
  V-55 433 kW as an era retrofit, D-10T stock with D-10T2S as a later-era sidegrade, 10-RT radio
  (era-correct: R-113 arrives with T-54A), 34-round honest ammo, module masses sum to 36,000 kg.
- Deliberate deviations, each with a reason, live in the program register
  (`docs/model-idealny-t54.md`): e.g. hitbox width band (M14, movement honesty decision pending),
  dispersion card semantics (out of program scope).

## Known deviations & follow-ups

The full measured register (M1–M14 dimensional, K1–K13 construction) with evidence and wave
assignments is maintained in **`docs/model-idealny-t54.md`** — the Model Idealny T-54 program
document. Headlines as of 2026-07-28: hull 6.00 vs 6.20–6.27; dome roof 2.27 vs 2.40; turret
side armor 90 vs 160→65; external mantlet ball vs narrow embrasure; cupola ⌀0.48 vs 0.624;
symmetric fenders; muzzle reads bore-less (wall 49 mm vs real 10–15); OMSh link lacks the guide
horn; sprocket teeth stop short of the belt; hatches are plain drums without hinges.

## Hybrid Production Path

The production T-54 is built through `vehicle_build::t54_description()` and selected by
`vehicle_forge::authoritative_baked_vehicle`; the older `vehicle_geometry` recipe is not a second
authoritative model. Hard hull armour, glacis, deck and fenders use `solid`; the cast turret shell,
cheeks and mantlet socket are a **cast loft** — a stack of designed horizontal stations skinned into
one watertight casting (`vehicle_build::t54_turret_loft`, kernel in the `loft` crate — renamed to
`cast_loft` in R1 of the procedural kernel program), which replaced the earlier metaball `sdf_mesh`
turret as of commit `2efa773`. SDF remains available for
other cast work (fluid unions, sockets), but the production turret shell is no longer `sdf_mesh`. The
barrel, mantlet, wheels, idler, sprocket and tracks use `revolve`. LOD0 is the clean
factory-condition garage asset, capped at 22,000 triangles with a 12,000-triangle cap for the cast
turret; battle LODs remain independently reduced.

A bake-time **ambient-contact pass** (R8 / Task 25) darkens `surface_shade` in the recesses a real
casting and welded hull show in ambient light — the turret-ring seam and undercut, the mantlet seat
in the embrasure, the running-gear/track recess under the sponson, and the glacis weld line. The
cavities are semantic `CavityBand`s derived from the same blueprint dimensions that drive the
geometry (`vehicle_build::t54_surface_bake`), applied to the merged submeshes at build time, so the
shading survives LOD and feeds the existing `surface_shade` render path with no renderer change. The
Forge manifest records the named signals and a config hash; the artifact `source_hash` already moves
with the resulting shade. Mud, rust, decals and camouflage stay runtime overlays.

## Damage-ready benchmark

- The cast turret collision ring uses 24 deterministic sectors while the production visual loft
  keeps 64 azimuth segments; contact tests keep the ballistic and rendered surfaces aligned.
- Permanent channels carry ammo-specific irregular outer/inner contours, age, impact energy,
  thickness, material and a stable semantic surface in hull, turret or moving-mantlet space.
  The 12-slot limit applies to physical perforation groups; ingress, egress and seam fragments from
  one shell share an id while retaining independent pose frames.
- The shared Forge asset remains intact. A bounded per-tank GPU descriptor cuts the opening in the
  color, camera-depth and shadow passes from the first frame. A worker builds a local constrained
  LOD0 patch and merges a 24-segment irregular paint lip, exposed-steel section and plate tunnel;
  invalid curved-casting patches are rejected and retain the analytical opening.
- The authored interior shares transforms with 16 physical damage components (u32 component mask
  as of protocol v27) and contains the breech/recoil group, turret drive, racks, fuel, V-54
  silhouette, cooling, transmission, final drives, radio and side-specific suspension.
  Through-flight hits are ordered physically rather than inferred from the armor-zone name.
- The cast turret carries a separate inward-facing primer skin, offset by roof/front/side/rear
  thickness bands. It closes the fighting compartment visually and is cut by the same aperture;
  the outer casting is never reused as an invisible one-sided interior wall.
- Interior materials receive sky and direct-sun energy only through at most four dominant aperture
  contours. Full armor cannot leak this light into the compartment.
- Thrown tracks expose a five-link gap at the replicated path position and close again on re-seat.
- Damage-mesh work records rolling worker/integration p95 telemetry and integrates at most one
  completed mesh upload per frame; analytical clipping remains visible while the worker is late.
